Coach's Notes

Growth Roadmap

Your Path Forward

Based on this month's patterns and your coaching goals.

Phase 1 — Days 1–14
Stop the Bleeding: Fix the Fundamentals
Eliminate every technical and structural mistake that is actively killing retention and discoverability. No new strategies until the floor is solid. Every upload from this point forward must pass a basic quality gate before going live.
Audit all existing Shorts and bulk-update titles using searchable keyword structures — replace vague titles like '…' and 'nuff said' with high-intent phrases such as 'Aerobatic pilot pulls 8Gs at 200mph' or 'What a snap roll looks like from inside the cockpit'
Create a pre-upload checklist: (1) First frame is bright, sharp, and action-forward — no dark or blurry openers, (2) Title contains at least one searchable keyword, (3) A CTA exists somewhere in the video
Re-edit the 2 near-invisible night videos using exposure correction and contrast boosting in post — reupload with corrected thumbnails and keyword-rich titles to salvage any residual traffic potential
Record a simple CTA template — a 1-second end-card with voiceover saying 'Subscribe if you fly or wish you did' — and add this asset to every future Short as a non-negotiable standard
Reverse-engineer the 27K top performer: document the exact structure — hook type, first visual, audio cue, tension beat timing, resolution moment — and turn it into a repeatable 11-second script framework for all future Shorts
Identify the 5 best unused raw clips in the existing footage library that open on high-contrast, motion-filled frames — these become the fast-follow content queue for Phase 2
Phase 2 — Days 15–60
Build the Machine: Systemize What Works
Take the proven 11-second pattern-interrupt framework and scale it into a consistent production system. Introduce intentional editing as a standard — not an occasional experiment. Test 3 distinct content pillars to find which one drives subscriber conversion, not just views.
Produce 3 Shorts per week using the documented 11-second framework: Beat 1 — disorienting or extreme visual hook in frames 1–2, Beat 2 — context drop ('this is a snap roll at 200mph'), Beat 3 — tension escalation (G-force, proximity, speed data overlay), Beat 4 — payoff or unexpected resolution. Never open on a static or dark frame again.
Establish 3 content pillars and rotate weekly: Pillar A — Raw danger/skill (closest to the 27K winner, pure aerobatic footage with data overlays), Pillar B — Education/myth-busting ('what pilots actually feel during a loop'), Pillar C — Behind-the-scenes human story ('why I chose aerobatics over airlines'). Track CTR and subscriber conversion rate per pillar separately.
Add intentional editing elements to every video as a baseline — glitch transitions on impact moments, speed-ramping on pull-outs, white-flash cuts on inverted maneuvers, and G-force or altitude text overlays synced to the most intense frame. These are not optional flourishes — they are the proven performance differentiators.
A/B test CTA placement across 6 videos: test 3 with mid-video CTA (at peak tension moment) and 3 with end-card CTA — measure which placement drives more subscribers per 1000 views and standardize the winner for the rest of the phase
Fix the night content problem permanently — either shoot night flights with supplemental cockpit lighting and a higher-bitrate camera setting, or reframe night content as silhouette/instrument-focused storytelling that makes darkness intentional rather than a technical failure
Post one 'process Short' per month showing the editing or flying prep behind a viral clip — this builds parasocial trust, performs well with algorithm because it recycles high-performing footage, and attracts subscribers who want more than highlight reels
Phase 3 — Days 61–90
Compound the Growth: Optimize for Subscribers, Not Just Views
Views without subscribers are a leaking bucket. Phase 3 shifts the primary KPI from view count to subscriber conversion rate. Use data from Phase 2 pillar testing to double down on the highest-converting content type, introduce one channel-level hook to drive follow-through, and begin building a content ecosystem where each Short pulls viewers toward wanting more.
Analyze Phase 2 data — identify which of the 3 pillars produced the highest subscriber-per-view ratio, not just the highest view count. Increase that pillar's posting frequency to 2x per week and reduce the lowest-converting pillar to once every 2 weeks
Create a channel-level hook series — a named, recurring Short format that viewers can anticipate, such as '60 Seconds of G-Force' or 'Cockpit Confessions.' A recognizable series title gives casual viewers a reason to subscribe so they don't miss the next installment — test 3 episodes and measure repeat viewer rate
Introduce a pinned comment CTA strategy — on every new Short, pin a comment within the first 30 minutes of posting that adds context or poses a question ('Drop a ✈️ if you've ever wondered what 8Gs actually feels like') to boost early engagement signals and improve algorithmic distribution
Build a 30-day content calendar for month 4 based entirely on what the data shows — no guessing, no vague titles, no static openers. Each planned video should have a working title with a target keyword, a defined pillar, a hook type selected from the validated framework, and a scheduled upload date
Set concrete 90-day benchmarks to evaluate against before month 4 planning: target 40K subscribers (78% growth), average Short retention above 75%, at least one Short crossing 100K views by deploying the proven framework at higher volume, and subscriber conversion rate of 1 new sub per 300 views across all Phase 3 content
